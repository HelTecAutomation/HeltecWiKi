The **RCC6** is a compact development board based on the **ESP32-C6 MCU**, featuring 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.0, and BLE connectivity. With a solder-free expansion design and support for Arduino, PlatformIO, and ESP-IDF, it provides a flexible and efficient platform for IoT development.

</div>}


## Product Characteristics

- ESP32-C6 MCU with integrated 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.0, and BLE.
- Supports Arduino, ESP-IDF, PlatformIO, and MicroPython.
- Compatible with Meshtastic, MeshCore, and Home Assistant ecosystems.
- Compact solder-free design for rapid integration.
