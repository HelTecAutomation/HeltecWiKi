The **RC32** is a compact development board based on the **ESP32-S3R8 MCU**, featuring Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.0, and BLE connectivity. With a solder-free expansion design and support for Arduino, PlatformIO, and ESP-IDF, it provides a flexible and efficient platform for IoT development.

  </a>
</div>}

## Product Characteristics

- ESP32-S3R8 with built-in Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.0, and BLE connectivity.
- Supports Arduino, ESP-IDF, PlatformIO, and MicroPython development environments.
- Compatible with Meshtastic, MeshCore, and Home Assistant ecosystems.
- Compact solder-free design for convenient expansion, prototyping, and rapid integration.
