**RC32** is a compact and modular system-level development platform based on the **ESP32-S3R8 MCU**. It supports flexible integration of multiple wireless communication modules, including **LoRa SX1262** and **Wi-Fi HaLow HC01**, enabling developers to select suitable connectivity solutions for different IoT application scenarios. With a variety of **deployment kits and accessories**, RC32 simplifies system integration and enables rapid prototyping and fast deployment of customized IoT solutions.


:::tip
**More Modules** may be added in the future, providing additional connectivity options for different application scenarios.
:::
