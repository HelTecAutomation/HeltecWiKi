The **RC3268** is a compact IoT development board featuring the **ESP32-S3R8 MCU** and **[HC01 Wi-Fi HaLow Module](https://heltec.org/project/ht-hc01-v2-wifi-halow-module/)**. It combines Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.0/BLE, and Wi-Fi HaLow connectivity, with support for both **AP Mode** and **Client Mode** to meet different application requirements. Compatible with Arduino, PlatformIO, and ESP-IDF, it enables rapid development of long-range IoT solutions.

## Product Characteristics

- Features an **ESP32-S3R8 MCU** and an **MM6108IQ Chip**, supporting Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.0/BLE, and HaLow connectivity.
- Supports HaLow **AP** and **Client** modes.
- Compatible with Arduino, ESP-IDF, and PlatformIO.
- Compact solder-free design for easy prototyping.
