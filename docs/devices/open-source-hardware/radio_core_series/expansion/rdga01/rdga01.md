**RD-GA01** is a portable expansion enclosure for **[RC32-62](/docs/devices/open-source-hardware/radio_core_series/rc32/rc3262/)** and **[RC52-62](/docs/devices/open-source-hardware/radio_core_series/rc52/rc5262/)**. It integrates a dedicated **enclosure**, **rotary switch**, **9-axis sensor**, **1800 mAh lithium battery**, and **display**, providing a compact and ready-to-use solution for IoT applications.

  </a>
</div>}


## Product Characteristics

- Includes a **display and rotary switch** for device control and menu navigation.
- Integrates a **9-axis sensor** for motion and orientation sensing, and an **1800 mAh lithium battery** for standalone operation.
- Equipped with a **dedicated enclosure** for an integrated and portable design.
