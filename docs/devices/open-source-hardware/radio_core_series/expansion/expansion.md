**RadioCore Expansion** extends the RadioCore platform with application-oriented handheld devices featuring **customized enclosures and built-in sensors**. These integrated devices are designed for specific application scenarios and provide a ready-to-use hardware foundation for development and prototyping.

The current series includes **[RD-GA01](/docs/devices/open-source-hardware/radio_core_series/expansion/rdga01/), [RD-GB01](/docs/devices/open-source-hardware/radio_core_series/expansion/rdgb01/), [RS-CP01](/docs/devices/open-source-hardware/radio_core_series/expansion/rscp01/), [RD01](/docs/devices/open-source-hardware/radio_core_series/expansion/rd01/),** and **[RDC1](/docs/devices/open-source-hardware/radio_core_series/expansion/rdc1/)**, each offering different hardware configurations and integrated sensing capabilities.
