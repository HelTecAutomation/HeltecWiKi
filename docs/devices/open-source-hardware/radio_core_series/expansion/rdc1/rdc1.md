The **RDC1** is a compact RadioCore-based node compatible with the **[RC32-62](/docs/devices/open-source-hardware/radio_core_series/rc32/rc3262/)** and **[RC52-62](/docs/devices/open-source-hardware/radio_core_series/rc52/rc5262/)**. It features a silicone enclosure, a small footprint, and excellent portability for easy carrying.

    Product Page
  </a>
</div>}

## Product Characteristics

- Compatible with RC32-62 and RC52-62
- Compact, portable design with a silicone enclosure and PCB antenna


## Important parameters

| parameters      | RDC1        |
|--------------------|----------------------------|
|Compatible RadioCore      |	    RC32-62, RC52-62              |
| Power Supply     |   		USB-C 5V              |
| Antenna          | 	PCB         |
| Enclosure Material            |  	Silicone |
| Dimensions         |   63.15 * 27.72 * 14.95mm	    |
