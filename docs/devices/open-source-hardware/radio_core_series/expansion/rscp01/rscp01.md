**RS-CP01** is a handheld expansion enclosure for the **[RC32-68](/docs/devices/open-source-hardware/radio_core_series/rc32/rc3268/)**, integrating a **Camera** and **PIR sensor** into a dedicated enclosure for **remote monitoring**, **motion detection**, and other IoT applications.

## Product Characteristics

- Features the ESP32-S3R8 MCU and HC01 Wi-Fi HaLow module, supporting Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.0/BLE, and HaLow connectivity.
- Supports HaLow AP and Client modes, with real-time video monitoring through the integrated camera.
- Includes an integrated PIR motion sensor and a dedicated enclosure for practical deployment.
- Compatible with Arduino, ESP-IDF, and PlatformIO for rapid development.
